How to Use Arreglo

1

Configure API

Click the settings icon and add your OpenAI API key. This is required for AI generation (until backend rollout).

2

Song Details

Enter your song's title, length (in bars), and genre. These help the AI understand the context.

3

Add Patterns

Click "Type in Patterns" to add your instruments (e.g., "kick - 4x", "hi-hat - offbeat"). You can also upload a DAW screenshot to extract track names.

4

Select Sections

Choose which sections you want in your arrangement (Intro, Verse, Chorus, etc.).

5

Adjust Creativity

Set the creativity level from traditional to experimental to control how innovative the arrangement will be.

6

Generate

Click "Make Arrangement" and watch as Arreglo creates your visual arrangement in Figma!
