Getting Started

To use Arreglo, sign in with your email address. You'll receive a magic link to verify your account. Each generation uses one credit from your account.

1

Sign In

Enter your email address and click the sign-in button to receive a magic link.

2

Song Details

Enter your song's title, length in bars, and genre. These help the AI understand the context.

3

Add Patterns

Click "Type in Patterns" to add your instruments (e.g., "kick - 4x", "hi-hat - offbeat"). You can also upload a DAW screenshot.

4

Select Sections

Choose which sections you want in your arrangement (Intro, Verse, Chorus, etc.).

5

Adjust Creativity

Set the creativity level to control how innovative the arrangement will be.

6

Generate

Click "Make Arrangement" and watch as Arreglo creates your visual arrangement in Figma!

Best Practices for Naming Patterns

  • Be specific: "kick - 4x", "snare - fills", "bassline - melody"
  • Use common instrument names (kick, snare, hi-hat, bass, pad, lead, etc.)
  • Describe the rhythmic or melodic role (e.g., "offbeat", "chords", "buildup")
  • Keep it short and clear; avoid unnecessary words
  • Each pattern should represent a single instrument or sound

Good pattern names help the AI understand your arrangement and generate more accurate results.

What the AI Does

Arreglo's AI analyzes all the information you provide to generate a complete, genre-appropriate arrangement. Here's how it works:

  • Pattern Analysis: The AI interprets your pattern names and roles to understand which instruments are present and how they typically behave in your genre.
  • Genre Intelligence: It applies genre-specific conventions for structure, energy, and instrument usage (e.g., House vs. Pop vs. DnB).
  • Section Mapping: Your selected sections (Intro, Verse, Chorus, etc.) are mapped to typical song structures, with transitions and energy arcs.
  • Creativity Control: The creativity slider lets you choose between traditional, balanced, or experimental arrangements.
  • Dynamic Arrangement: The AI balances repetition and variation, ensuring your arrangement is both logical and interesting.
  • Export-Ready: The result is a visual arrangement you can export as MIDI or use as a reference in your DAW.

The more clearly you describe your patterns and song details, the better the AI can generate a musical arrangement that fits your vision.
